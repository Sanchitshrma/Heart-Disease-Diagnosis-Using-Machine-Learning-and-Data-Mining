Feature guidance

Click each item to view an expanded description of the clinical input fields used by the model.

Age

Patient age in years. This feature captures age-related risk because cardiovascular disease incidence tends to increase with older age.

Sex

Sex is encoded as 1 for male and 0 for female. The model uses this to account for observed differences in heart disease prevalence and symptom presentation.

Chest Pain Type

The type of chest pain experienced by the patient. Categories range from typical angina and atypical angina to non-anginal pain and asymptomatic presentation.

Resting Blood Pressure

Resting systolic blood pressure measured in mm Hg. High values may indicate hypertension, which is a significant cardiovascular risk factor.

Cholesterol

Serum cholesterol measured in mg/dL. Higher cholesterol is linked to plaque buildup in arteries and increased coronary risk.

Fasting Blood Sugar

Binary indicator for fasting blood sugar above 120 mg/dL. Elevated values suggest impaired glucose regulation, which is correlated with heart disease risk.

Resting ECG

Resting electrocardiogram result coded as normal, ST-T abnormality, or left ventricular hypertrophy. Abnormal ECG findings can signal underlying cardiac stress.

Max Heart Rate

Maximum heart rate achieved during exercise testing. Lower peak heart rate may indicate reduced cardiovascular fitness or blockages in coronary circulation.

Exercise Induced Angina

Whether chest pain occurred during exercise. This binary feature helps identify exertional ischemia, a key marker for coronary artery disease.

Oldpeak

ST depression induced by exercise relative to rest. Higher oldpeak values indicate greater stress on the heart during exercise.

ST Slope

Slope of the ST segment during peak exercise. Different slope patterns are associated with varying levels of cardiac risk.

FAQs
Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. This model provides a risk screening estimate only and should not replace professional medical advice or clinical evaluation.

What does the risk score mean?

The risk score is the probability that the model assigns to heart disease presence, based on the submitted clinical values.

How should I prepare inputs?

Provide the most recent clinical measurements available and select encoded categories according to the patient’s current status.

Can I use this app in production?

This interface is a demonstration. A production deployment should include model validation, clinical review, data security, and regulatory compliance.

Why are these features included?

The selected features are commonly used in heart disease prediction because they reflect established risk factors for coronary artery disease.

How accurate is the model?

The model is trained on the dataset included in this repository. Accuracy can vary with new patient populations and should be externally validated.

What if a value is missing?

All fields are required in this application. If a clinical measurement is unavailable, obtain the missing data before submitting the form.
